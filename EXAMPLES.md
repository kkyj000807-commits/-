## Tips for Effective Learning

1. **Start with Study Mode** - Familiarize yourself with all words first
2. **Use Quiz Mode regularly** - Test yourself to reinforce learning
3. **Focus on one difficulty level** - Master easier words before moving up
4. **Review repeatedly** - Spaced repetition is key to retention
5. **Use the words** - Try to use new vocabulary in your writing and speaking
